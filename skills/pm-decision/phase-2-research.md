# Phase 2 — Research & Evidence

**Goal:** evidence base before shaping. Don't skip for "obvious" ideas.

## Research streams (propose via AskUserQuestion, multiSelect)
- Client signals (requests, tickets, churn)
- Workflow observation (how solved today, workarounds)
- Competitive landscape
- Internal capability audit (what we have, what's reusable)
- Volume & frequency
- Proxy data (usage, time-on-task, errors, re-work)

## Evidence quality bar
| Quality | Example |
|---|---|
| Strong | "3 clients cancelled citing this exact gap" |
| Acceptable | "Mentioned in 8 of last 12 onboarding calls" |
| Weak | "We assume clients want this" |
| Invalid | "It would be cool if…" |

Push back on weak evidence. Don't proceed to shaping on assumptions.

**This bar is not decorative — it becomes a number.** Phase 3 scores every epic with RICE on a 1-5
scale, and the Confidence factor is read straight off this table: strong → 5, acceptable → 3, weak →
1. So an epic resting on an assumption is mathematically demoted rather than merely flagged, and
inflating confidence later means arguing with evidence you wrote down yourself.

## Competitive analysis (per analogue)
- How do they frame the problem?
- Solution architecture?
- What they get right / miss?
- What our version must do differently to win?

## Done when
Evidence is grounded. You can answer "How do we know this is real?" with specifics.
